## Suggested clarification: Agent Deck communications are bounded records

**Nonblocking completeness improvement, affecting F11/F13/F14/F19 and adoption advice.** The report accurately describes a durable comms ledger and does not explicitly promise permanent/full-text history. It should nevertheless mention its truncation and retention limits before readers use it as Wingman room history or an authoritative project archive.

[record.go](../../../agent-deck/repo/internal/comms/record.go), lines 62–67, declares a 600-byte default and 2048-byte ceiling; lines 205–225 implement rune-safe clipping with a visible ellipsis. This is used in the production turn ingestion path: [comms_ingest.go](../../../agent-deck/repo/internal/session/comms_ingest.go), lines 301–305, caps both turn text and completion summary. [ledger.go](../../../agent-deck/repo/internal/comms/ledger.go), lines 19–36, declares 90-day audit retention, 1024 sealed segments and a 2 GiB per-profile disk bound; lines 211–215 supply those limits and protect unacknowledged segments for recently reading consumers. [consumer.go](../../../agent-deck/repo/internal/comms/consumer.go), lines 43–47 and 433–443, explicitly permits compaction beyond an inactive consumer's watermark and reports the resulting gap. These protections are stronger than an ephemeral event log, but are not an indefinite conversation archive.

Suggested addition to the coordination or persistence discussion: “Ledger text is bounded (600 bytes by default, 2048-byte ceiling), and audit segments have retention/disk limits. Recently active unacknowledged consumers protect pending delivery; stale readers can receive explicit history gaps. Preserve full conversations, decisions and artifacts separately when building Wingman's durable rooms and project knowledge.”
