## Edict: independently verified boundaries

**Identity and collaboration (F01/F02/F11/F24).** [Installer](../../../edict/repo/install.sh), lines 95–118 and 138–150, provisions eleven separate workspaces, installs SOUL instructions, registers allowed delegation relationships, and explicitly directs interdepartment cooperation through Shangshu. This supports real registered agents, while denying that the court UI proves direct peer collaboration. [Court discussion](../../../edict/repo/dashboard/court_discuss.py), lines 112–144, stores sessions in a module dictionary. Lines 173–181 select LLM or simulated generation. Lines 533–574 ask one model call to generate every official's next message. The report correctly calls these personas and volatile sessions.

**Telegram (F04).** [Telegram adapter](../../../edict/repo/edict/backend/app/channels/telegram.py), lines 11–40, validates an outbound webhook and POSTs formatted text. It has no inbound update receiver, project matching or answer correlation. Searches across dashboard/scripts/backend/frontend for Telegram and intake/routing terms found dispatch-channel selection and notification adapters, rather than a Wingman clarification loop. The report's Resembles is appropriately limited.

**Real supervision with a freshness defect (F15).** [Scheduler](../../../edict/repo/dashboard/server.py), lines 1042–1054, initializes `lastProgressAt`. Lines 1283–1288 prefer it over task `updatedAt`. Lines 1300–1382 mutate retry/escalation/rollback state and dispatch/wake after releasing the lock. [Progress update](../../../edict/repo/scripts/kanban_update.py), lines 632–640, appends progress and updates only `updatedAt`. Thus the report's false-stall concern is backed by the actual read/write mismatch. Lines 1336–1350 of the scheduler also corroborate that same-state snapshots do not advance the rollback counter.

**UI summaries and acceptance (F18/F19/F22).** [Official detail](../../../edict/repo/edict/frontend/src/components/OfficialPanel.tsx), lines 117–167, builds heartbeat/usage/task counters, rather than a maintained current/recent/next narrative. [Progress command](../../../edict/repo/scripts/kanban_update.py), lines 607–640, provides much better task-scoped semantic evidence, but not the cross-task agent feed. [Done handler](../../../edict/repo/scripts/kanban_update.py), lines 440–467, explicitly gates existing todos and moves to Review; this does not itself run tests or establish a product DoD. The report discloses generic-transition and child-completion bypasses instead of asserting a universal gate.

**Alternate-stack request wiring.** [Orchestrator](../../../edict/repo/edict/backend/app/workers/orchestrator_worker.py), lines 166–225, publishes sparse dispatch payloads containing task ID/agent/state/message; [dispatcher](../../../edict/repo/edict/backend/app/workers/dispatch_worker.py), lines 382–429, feeds those payloads directly to context/memory/skill helpers and invokes OpenClaw without fetching the database task there. [Compose](../../../edict/repo/edict/docker-compose.yml) declares orchestrator/dispatcher services but no outbox relay. [Frontend API](../../../edict/repo/edict/frontend/src/api.ts), lines 27–35, requests top-level compatibility endpoints; [FastAPI mounts](../../../edict/repo/edict/backend/app/main.py), lines 66–72, place the task/legacy routers below `/api/tasks`. The report correctly refuses to combine the default and rewrite stacks into one proven end-to-end implementation.

The lifecycle section covers all guide concerns, including permissions/fail-open policy, registration versus task invocation, concurrency, memory, retry, state-only stop, review, archive and uninstall. Major extras cover operational departments, skills, news, notifications, advisory deliberation and separate plan review. Innovation discussion explains mechanisms and integration limits without historical novelty claims.

## Claw-Empire: independently verified boundaries

**Native Telegram path versus enriched directives (F04/F05/request trace).** [Receiver](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/messenger/telegram-receiver.ts), lines 306–347, filters allowed non-bot text and forwards source/message/author/chat/text. It sends no `project_id`. [Default policy](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/routes/ops/messages.ts), line 10, enables project-binding enforcement; [inbox](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/routes/ops/messages/directives-inbox-routes.ts), lines 490–503, returns 428 for directives lacking that ID. The ordinary-message branch instead resolves a mapped employee at lines 506–540. [Direct-chat binding](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/routes/collab/direct-chat-handlers.ts), lines 492–537, records a pending binding and asks existing/new. The companion correctly traces the latter path conditionally, rather than pretending a native raw `$` message reaches execution under defaults.

**Chat is not transparent live peer messaging (F11).** [Chat routes](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/routes/ops/messages/chat-routes.ts), lines 41–82 and 105–127, persist arbitrary sender/receiver messages. The automatic reasoning reply/delegation block at lines 201–245 is entered for CEO→agent messages. [Planning](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/workflow/orchestration/planned-approval.ts), lines 213–295, separately calls selected leader personas one turn at a time and persists their speech. Lines 302–333 convert actions into a memo and callback. This substantiates the report's limited Similar judgment, not autonomous project rooms among continuing workers.

**Native execution/continuity (F01/F14/F17/F21).** [CLI argument builder](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/workflow/core/cli-tools.ts), lines 56–64, launches fresh Codex `exec --json` and passes no native thread/resume argument. [Execution start](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/workflow/orchestration/execution-start-task.ts), lines 129–170, always creates its worktree; [worktree lifecycle](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/workflow/core/worktree/lifecycle.ts), lines 162–188, removes existing candidate directories before re-adding a branch. Thus a workflow-level resume is not proof of preserved uncommitted files or a native resumed Codex conversation. Host/SSH/remote-runner searches in server execution paths found no distributed agent control plane.

**Supervision and executive truth (F15/F18/F19).** [Lifecycle timers](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/lifecycle.ts), lines 455–464, do start orphan/delegation sweeps and native receivers. Conversely, [progress timer](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/workflow/orchestration/progress-notify-tools.ts), lines 9–38, reads only task status and emits a leader-authored claim that work is going smoothly. It neither consults tools/tests/blockers nor requests that leader's reasoning. The report's warning against using this as executive truth is required and correct.

**Completion despite failed integration (F22).** [Finalizer](../../../claw-empire/repo/server/modules/workflow/orchestration/review-finalize-tools.ts), lines 538–605, handles merge success/failure; failure logs and notifies but does not return. Line 608 unconditionally updates the task to `done`. Source-wide server search for `requireTestEvidence`/`enforceTests` found only development-pack declarations, not generic enforcement. The report therefore correctly separates actual specialized video gates, leader review and merge attempts from enforced software acceptance.

Its master plus companion cover definition/configuration/creation, reusable identity, provider execution differences, routing, capacity, tools, scheduling, interruption, recovery, completion and retirement in substantive detail. Major extras and innovation sections adequately cover multi-domain packs, video/report production, provider/account operations, skill learning, GitHub delivery, persistent meeting-to-work transitions and correlated messenger decisions. No additional major omission emerged from this audit.

## ClawTeam: independently verified boundaries

**Real workers and incomplete transactional launch (F01/F14/F16/F23).** [Spawn CLI](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/cli/commands.py), lines 3132–3148, generates a fresh instance ID and guards duplicate live names. Lines 3219–3245 register membership while ignoring an already-member error. Lines 3269–3282 add a saved native resume command, and 3298–3325 launch/rollback. In contrast, template launch lines 4176–4198 collect backend results and report `launched` without rejecting returned `Error` strings. The report explicitly discloses this weaker team-launch path rather than transferring individual-spawn rollback guarantees to it.

**Communication and acknowledgment (F11).** [Mailbox parsing](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/team/mailbox.py), lines 204–227, acknowledges a claimed message immediately after schema validation, before the model acts on it. [Collector](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/board/collector.py), lines 118–139, separately reads permanent message events. Real direct communication and durable evidence are present; end-to-end execution acknowledgment and a configurable human chat room product are not established. The report preserves this boundary.

**Actual optional supervision (F15/F18).** [LeaderWatcher](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/team/leader_watcher.py), lines 40–82, defines the 60-second poll/300-second heartbeat and explicit controller loop. Lines 87–158 implement change/heartbeat notification. Lines 191–235 derive a signature and templated counts from completed/blocked tasks, mail and dead agents. Lines 238–277 inject into the leader or queue its mailbox. This is useful supervision infrastructure, with a real reasoning worker expected to decide the next action. The report accurately says ordinary spawn/launch does not start this separate controller.

**Messaging across hosts is not remote process control (F17).** [P2P transport](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/transport/p2p.py), lines 28–34, relies on shared-file peer discovery. Lines 56–64 bind TCP. Lines 100–109 advertise host/port/PID/lease. Lines 146–179 distinguish local PID checking from remote lease checking. Lines 207–219 send through ZeroMQ and fall back to a file on observed failure. This is genuine cross-host messaging machinery, but no remote launch/workspace/stop scheduler follows from it. Successful socket enqueue also bypasses the durable recipient-file path, as the report states.

**Executive views (F18/F19/F20).** [Collector](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/board/collector.py), lines 77–109, produces member/inbox records and grouped tasks. Lines 118–139 produce message history. These supply raw evidence, not per-agent synthesized recent/current/next outcomes or a connected coding-session workspace. Searches for timeline/rooms/Telegram in the implementation found no missing matching product surface.

**Harness request wiring and acceptance (F07/F08/F12/F22).** [Default gates](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/harness/orchestrator.py), lines 55–61, gate plan on artifact/approval registration and verify on completed team tasks. [Phase implementation](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/harness/phases.py), lines 64–103, checks only registrations/statuses, and lines 109–115 and 187–192 create an empty gate map on load. [Orchestrator load](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/harness/orchestrator.py), lines 120–135, bypasses constructor/default registration. [Conductor](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/harness/conductor.py), lines 97–149, repeatedly advances its existing object without reloading separate approvals and declares completion upon reaching the final phase. [Approval CLI](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/cli/commands.py), lines 4487–4495, loads and updates a different object. These corroborate the report's central integration concerns; named phases do not establish completed discovery, verification or human-accepted delivery.

**Exit and cessation boundaries.** [Conductor](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/harness/conductor.py), lines 63–67, reads a run-specific exit journal; [exit CLI](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/cli/commands.py), lines 2943–2954, writes a team-only journal and clears native session references; [journal paths](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/harness/exit_journal.py), lines 20–24, differ. [Shutdown approval](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/team/lifecycle.py), lines 34–46, goes to the requester, while [keepalive test](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/cli/commands.py), lines 3017–3026, checks the exiting worker's own inbox. The report correctly identifies these integration gaps rather than presenting the message protocol as guaranteed process cessation.

**Extras and innovation coverage.** The report's snapshot boundary is independently supported by [snapshot capture](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/team/snapshot.py), lines 123–183: its bundle enumerates config/tasks/events/session references/costs/inboxes, not source branches or live conversations. Its hook caveat is supported by [task creation](../../../clawteam/repo/clawteam/store/file.py), lines 99–121: `BeforeTaskCreate` is emitted asynchronously after persistence. Heterogeneous runtimes, Git collaboration context, snapshots, MCP, hooks/plugins, non-software templates and advisory budgets are substantive extras. The innovation discussion explains mechanisms and limits without claiming global novelty.

## Optional consistency note

F19 is rated Resembles for Edict and Claw-Empire, but Absent for ClawTeam. All three correctly deny the full requested semantic agent timeline, while retaining raw task/message/commit history. These are defensible scoped judgments, so I would not block completion on them. If the overall collection needs rigid cross-report calibration, use one policy: either Resembles for an attributed activity-history primitive, or Absent for the synthesized timeline capability. Preserve the written boundaries whichever label is chosen.
