What Wingman must deliver

One person directs a persistent software-development organization through conversation.

Butler receives Telegram requests. ProjectMaster identifies the product and supervises progress. ProductMaster defines the product. TechMaster assigns technical work. Independent specialists implement and verify it.

The organization supports parallel products and work across hosts. Shared records connect tasks, conversations, decisions, specifications, and deliverables. The executive view shows recent results, current work, next work, and blockers.

Read the full Wingman purpose →

High-impact findings

Keep organization and execution separate. Durable projects, role ownership, decisions, and acceptance need explicit records. A coding session cannot supply all of them.

Study several foundations. Paperclip, OpenRig, Multica, Crewly, and Agent Swarm provide useful organization mechanisms. Their workflow boundaries differ.

Protect continuity and ownership. Separate agent, session, task, and attempt identity. Reject stale results. Distinguish delivery from handling and acceptance.

Build the product journey. Connect discovery, specifications, technical assignments, integrated verification, and human acceptance. Keep that evidence after delivery.
